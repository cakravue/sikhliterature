# Ras 2, Ansu 22

## The Guruship given to Sri Arjan Ji; the fourth pad of the shabad composed

*Dohra*

**[1]** Joy rose in the Satguru's heart as he heard the Elder[^1] out. *Buddha has praised him, and
with that Sri Arjan has become the moon of the lineage.*

*Chaupai*

**[2]** *The Guruship has been carried on many days now, and in that time he has gained the eye that
tells substance from worthlessness. When the chief jeweller appraises a jewel, the world learns what
it is worth.*[^2]

**[3-5]** A coconut and five paise (the customary offering at an installation) were sent for, and
Sikhs called in from wherever they were. The whole saṅgat gathered and closed around him on all four
sides. Everyone was watching. *What will he do? Will he hold anger in his heart against the eldest
son?* Then Sri Ram Das stood, took the coconut and the coins in his hand, and walked three times
round his son, keeping him on his right, and so recognised Sri Arjan as worthy. He set the offering
down before him and touched his forehead to the ground. There was great joy; it was an ocean of vivek
(discernment).

**[6]** Then, at the Satguru's command, Buddha rose with a glad heart and set the tilak (the mark
that installs a man in office) with his own hand on that fine forehead, filled as it was with
fortune.

**[7-9]** The Satguru spoke to them all. "Listen, Sikhs: you are all dear to the Guru. Look on Sri
Arjan as my own form, and hold him in your heart exactly as you hold me. He is settled now on the
seat of Guruship, and the whole burden of this world and the next is laid on him; he will carry the
saṅgat's suffering across, and accomplish many works in the world. Rise, all of you, and bow to him,
and give offerings of every kind." At his words the Sikhs rejoiced and called out "Jai! Jai!" aloud.

**[10]** Each man set gifts before him as his means allowed, and bowed with folded hands. There was
celebration among all the Sikhs, and they looked on Sri Arjan as the rising sun.

**[11-13]** The Guru's servants opened like a bed of lotuses, and the seekers were delighted, a swarm
of bees. But the hope in the hearts of Mahadev and Prithi[^3] was a night thick with the darkness of
delusion, and that night was destroyed. Their partisans hid themselves away like stars, and the
slanderers shut their eyes and their mouths like owls. Prithi Chand's brightness had been the moon's;
in that very instant it went pale and dim. He burned, and suffered greatly, and bent his neck, and
nothing was in his power. Mahadev stayed silent and unmoved, and let neither gladness nor grief into
his heart.

**[14-15]** When the mother[^4] heard, she knew herself greatly fortunate. *Arjan has become Guru.*
Her delight was great, and her father's words came back to her and settled her. *The Guruship will
not pass out of the lineage. What my father the Guru said is unshakeable. Sumeru[^5] may move; that
will not move.* Then Sri Satguru took his seat in the assembly, the whole saṅgat around him on all
four sides.

**[16-18]** He turned to his son Sri Arjan, his mind especially glad, and spoke. "A shabad has four
pads (stanzas) in all. Three are made; one is wanted now. Compose a fine pad here beside me, and keep
your mind steady as you bring the shabad to its completion, so that many Sikhs in the world will read
it and hear it and take great joy in it." The Elder, glad to hear it, said, "The Satguru's word is
fitting indeed." Sri Arjan bowed to his father there and then, and composed the pad.

> *Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Raag Majh, Mahalla 4; Ang 96 to 97. The fourth pad, by Sri Guru Arjan Dev
> Ji, completing Guru Ram Das Ji's shabad ਮੇਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਲੋਚੈ ਗੁਰ ਦਰਸਨ ਤਾਈ.*
>
> ਭਾਗੁ ਹੋਆ ਗੁਰਿ ਸੰਤੁ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ ॥\
> *bhāg hoā gur sant milāiā*\
> Good fortune came, and the Guru brought me to the Sant.[^6]
>
> ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਅਬਿਨਾਸੀ ਘਰ ਮਹਿ ਪਾਇਆ ॥\
> *prabh abināsī ghar meh pāiā*\
> I found the imperishable Prabhu[^7] within my own house.
>
> ਸੇਵ ਕਰੀ ਪਲੁ ਚਸਾ ਨ ਵਿਛੁੜਾ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸ ਤੁਮਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ ॥੪॥\
> *sev karī pal chasā na vichhuṛā jan nānak dās tumāre jīu 4*\
> I do seva, and am not parted from you for a moment, not for an instant; Nanak your servant, your slave.
>
> ਹਉ ਘੋਲੀ ਜੀਉ ਘੋਲਿ ਘੁਮਾਈ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸ ਤੁਮਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ ॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥੧॥੮॥\
> *hau gholī jīu ghol ghumāī jan nānak dās tumāre jīu rahāu 1 8*\
> I am a sacrifice, my soul a sacrifice; Nanak your servant, your slave.

*Chaupai*

**[19-21]** Sri Guru Ram Das was gladdened to hear it, and judged: *He will compose a great body of
sacred verse.* He saw that the making of it was fine and clear, and that it had come at once, with no
delay taken. Then he instructed his son from his own mouth. "Make the shabad with humility, so that
whoever reads it, hears it and reflects on it has his pride taken away, and his vices with it." Sri
Arjan folded his hands as he listened, gave himself over wholly, and bowed. "You alone are the doer
and the cause of doing. I follow always as you say."

**[22]** The assembly rose, and the men went off to their own places saying, "This is good." Prithia
met Bhai the Elder. He was burning, and nothing was in his power.

**[23-25]** "Listen, Buddha. We knew you. We held your greatness equal to Guru Nanak's, and that is
what my mind has been until today; no faith is left in me now that I have seen what you did. You
stayed with the Satguru through all of it, and watched the whole account from the beginning. I am the
eldest, and capable besides, and the Guruship after my father was rightly mine. You considered none
of it. You have grown old, and the strength of your mind has failed. My father was Guru in name
alone; it was I who ran the greater part of the affairs.

**[26-27]** "You know what I am capable of in every kind of matter. The whole saṅgat took me for the
Guru," he said. "What is this deed you have suddenly done? You rose and gave the tilak on the spot.
If I overturn it by my own strength, what honour of yours will be left? There was never any mention
of this before. Why did you do so improper a thing, and so suddenly?

**[28-30]** "If my father resolved on this in anger, and agreed to follow an improper practice, even
then it was for you to counsel him and speak good sense to him: 'This right belongs to the eldest
son, and he is capable in every way besides. All the father's affairs pass through him, and he
manages them with a generous good sense.' You should have said such things and counselled him, and
kept the Guruship from the younger son. That was the proper course for you, and every mind would have
thought you wise for it.

**[31-33]** "That good thing you did not do. You rose and gave the tilak on the instant. Will the
wise Sikhs of the saṅgat not condemn what you did? I have seen your conduct now. They call you a man
of sense, and you have done an unseemly thing. From now on I will make constant effort, and I will
snatch the seat away from him. I will spend wealth in quantity and take it, and what all of you have
done I will overturn."

**[33-36]** The Elder answered him. "Why hold anger in your heart? You put no love into your father's
seva. Had he been pleased with you, he would have given it to you unasked, if he had seen you worthy
of it. Backbiting will not bring it to your hand; take hold of humility, and you get what you want.
You did not properly accept your father's word, and that is why the tilak was not yours. You are a
Guru's son; great is your greatness; you are worthy of honour. What of it, if it did not come to
hand? Leave anger and quarrelling, and find ease. Gladden your heart in whatever your father does. He
is master of faith and of the world; who can answer him to his face?"

**[37]** With that the Elder fell silent. Prithia went into the house, and speaking harsh words and
holding his anger, he came before his father, heavy with grievance.

**[38-41]** Bitter words began to come out of him. "What contrary thing have you resolved on? You
have given what is mine to the younger. Who does such a thing? It has not happened before. You did it
thinking ill of me. What fault did you see, that you cast me aside? You will see the fruit of what
you have done to me in what comes. Wealth in quantity is close at hand for me now. I will join
whichever king is powerful, and make him my own by giving him wealth, and show you suffering, father
and son both. When nothing at all is in your power, the Guruship will be mine without my lifting a
hand." He raised much quarrelling of this kind, and to that Sri Guru uttered a shabad.

> *Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Raag Sarang, Mahalla 4, Ghar 3, Dupada; Ang 1200.*
>
> ਸਾਰਗ ਮਹਲਾ ੪ ਘਰੁ ੩ ਦੁਪਦਾ\
> ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥\
> *sārag mahalā 4 gharu 3 dupadā · ik oaṅkār satigur prasād*
>
> ਕਾਹੇ ਪੂਤ ਝਗਰਤ ਹਉ ਸੰਗਿ ਬਾਪ ॥\
> *kāhe pūt jhagrat hau saṅg bāp*\
> Why do you quarrel with your father, son?
>
> ਜਿਨ ਕੇ ਜਣੇ ਬਡੀਰੇ ਤੁਮ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਸਿਉ ਝਗਰਤ ਪਾਪ ॥੧॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥\
> *jin ke jaṇe badīre tum hau, tin siu jhagrat pāp 1 rahāu*\
> He fathered you and brought you up; to quarrel with him is a sin.
>
> ਜਿਸੁ ਧਨ ਕਾ ਤੁਮ ਗਰਬੁ ਕਰਤ ਹਉ ਸੋ ਧਨੁ ਕਿਸਹਿ ਨ ਆਪ ॥\
> *jis dhan kā tum garab karat hau, so dhan kiseh na āp*\
> The wealth you take pride in belongs to no one.
>
> ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਛੋਡਿ ਜਾਇ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਰਸੁ ਤਉ ਲਾਗੈ ਪਛੁਤਾਪ ॥੧॥\
> *khin meh chhod jāi bikhiā ras, tau lāgai pachhutāp 1*\
> In an instant the relish of that poison leaves you, and then regret sets in.
>
> ਜੋ ਤੁਮਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਹੋਤੇ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਹਰਿ ਤਿਨ ਕੇ ਜਾਪਹੁ ਜਾਪ ॥\
> *jo tumre prabh hote suāmī har, tin ke jāpahu jāp*\
> Hari,[^8] who was your Prabhu and your master: say his name over.
>
> ਉਪਦੇਸੁ ਕਰਤ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਨ ਤੁਮ ਕਉ ਜਉ ਸੁਨਹੁ ਤਉ ਜਾਇ ਸੰਤਾਪ ॥੨॥੧॥੭॥\
> *updes karat nānak jan tum kau, jau sunahu tau jāi saṅtāp 2 1 7*\
> Nanak the servant gives you this teaching: if you listen, your torment goes.

*Chaupai*

**[42-43]** "Prithia, listen. What pride is there in wealth, which forsakes a man and will not stay
an instant for anyone? Regret is what is left behind, and quarrelling with a father brings no honour.
So repeat the Name of Hari, Hari, and hold to this good teaching. It destroys every torment of body
and mind, and greed and grave sin take no hold again."

**[44-46]** That galled Prithia worse than before. "'Repeat the Name of Hari, Hari, Gupal',[^9] you
tell me, having given the Guruship to the younger son and dishonoured me in front of everyone. You
see me as fit only to be preached at, and you have picked a fine moment to speak: 'Put the hope of
Guruship out of your heart, and worship Hari, and be a servant.' I shall take up this teaching of
yours! Fine work you will make of carrying me across the ocean of existence! You do what is unfit and
consider nothing, and now it is 'Repeat the Name of Hari, Hari.'"

**[47]** Sri Ram Das heard the crooked words. *He will not turn back, whoever tries to stop him. His
one love is in the doing of wrong.* Then he composed a second shabad and recited it.

> *Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Raag Suhi, Mahalla 4; Ang 733.*
>
> ਸੂਹੀ ਮਹਲਾ ੪ ॥\
> *sūhī mahalā 4*
>
> ਤਿਨ੍ਹੀ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਆਰਾਧਿਆ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਧੁਰਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਲਿਖਤੁ ਲਿਲਾਰਾ ॥\
> *tinhī antar har ārādhiā, jin kau dhur likhiā likhat lilārā*\
> They have worshipped Hari within, on whose foreheads it was written from the beginning.
>
> ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਬਖੀਲੀ ਕੋਈ ਕਿਆ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਨ ਕਾ ਅੰਗੁ ਕਰੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਕਰਤਾਰਾ ॥੧॥\
> *tin kī bakhīlī koī kiā kare, jin kā aṅg kare merā har kartārā 1*\
> What can anyone's backbiting do to them, whose side my Hari the Creator takes?
>
> ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਇ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਧਿਆਇ ਹਰਿ ਜਨਮ ਜਨਮ ਕੇ ਸਭਿ ਦੂਖ ਨਿਵਾਰਣਹਾਰਾ ॥੧॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥\
> *har har dhiāi man mere, man dhiāi har janam janam ke sabh dūkh nivāraṇhārā 1 rahāu*\
> Meditate on Hari, my mind; meditate, my mind, on Hari, the remover of the sufferings of birth after birth.
>
> ਧੁਰਿ ਭਗਤ ਜਨਾ ਕਉ ਬਖਸਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਭਗਤਿ ਭੰਡਾਰਾ ॥\
> *dhur bhagat janā kau bakhsiā har amrit bhagti bhaṇdārā*\
> From the beginning Hari granted his devotees the amrit treasury of devotion.
>
> ਮੂਰਖੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੁ ਉਨ ਕੀ ਰੀਸ ਕਰੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਹਲਤਿ ਪਲਤਿ ਮੁਹੁ ਕਾਰਾ ॥੨॥\
> *mūrakh hovai su un kī rīs kare, tis halat palat muhu kārā 2*\
> Whoever competes with them is a fool; his face is blackened in this world and the next.
>
> ਸੇ ਭਗਤ ਸੇ ਸੇਵਕਾ ਜਿਨਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ ॥\
> *se bhagat se sevkā, jinā har nām piārā*\
> They are the devotees, they are the servants, to whom Hari's Name is dear.
>
> ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਿਰਿ ਨਿੰਦਕ ਕੈ ਪਵੈ ਛਾਰਾ ॥੩॥\
> *tin kī sevā te har pāīai, sir nindak kai pavai chhārā 3*\
> Through their seva Hari is obtained, and ashes fall on the slanderer's head.
>
> ਜਿਸੁ ਘਰਿ ਵਿਰਤੀ ਸੋਈ ਜਾਣੈ ਜਗਤ ਗੁਰ ਨਾਨਕ ਪੂਛਿ ਕਰਹੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਾ ॥\
> *jis ghar virtī soī jāṇai, jagat gur nānak pūchh karahu bīchārā*\
> He in whose house it works, he alone knows it; ask Nanak, Guru of the world, and reflect.
>
> ਚਹੁ ਪੀੜੀ ਆਦਿ ਜੁਗਾਦਿ ਬਖੀਲੀ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਇਓ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਵਕ ਭਾਇ ਨਿਸਤਾਰਾ ॥੪॥੨॥੯॥\
> *chahu pīṛī ād jugād bakhīlī kinai na pāio, har sevak bhāi nistārā 4 2 9*\
> Through four generations, from the beginning of the ages, no one has gained by backbiting; deliverance comes by the servant's way.

*Chaupai*

**[48-51]** "The love of Hari is fixed within the man whose good fortune is written from the
beginning, and Sri Arjan's helper, always, is Hari the Creator, lord of the whole world. What
backbiting can you do against a man in whom that sweet love of Hari is fixed? The devotee's blessing
too was seen from the beginning, and the amrit treasury of devotion is his to use. Whoever competes
with such men is a fool, and his face is blackened in this world and the next. The man to whom Hari's
Name and devotion are dear is a servant, and it is through such men that the light of Prabhu is
obtained. Nothing comes to the slanderer's hand. Ashes fall on his head, and afterwards he repents.
The man in whose heart the love of Hari is at work is the one who knows the Satguru's greatness.

**[52-53]** "Sri Nanak; Guru Angad, the moon; Sri Guru Amar Das, the root of ease: the fourth
generation of the Guruship has reached me, and all the ages have watched it. Backbiting did not bring
it to your hand. The supreme state is gained by doing seva, and seva never once came near you. You
were filled with pride, and your mind was steeped in deceit.

**[54-55]** "You took me for a man of little knowledge. You practised deceit and held yourself great.
Out of envy you took the letters[^10] and hid them away, as an enemy would. We questioned you again
and again, and you would not admit it; fearless and ill-minded, you swore an oath on it. You counted
the fault of a false oath as nothing, and went on taking me for a man who did not know.

**[56-58]** "Without humility there is no seva.[^11] Where is seva here? There is only great
egotism. Faith is the great root of it all, and love the sprout that grows from it and gives ease;
seva is the bark that makes the tree firm, vairāg (detachment) its great branches spreading wide,
Hari's Name the flowers that blossom out of it, and in the true saṅgat it sways, and is beautiful.
The many virtues of divine wealth are its leaves, a mass of virtue strong with vivek, and knowledge
of the self hangs on it as beautiful fruit, with great joy for the juice inside.

**[59-60]** "Ten million wishing-trees[^12] are not its equal, and a man finds it within himself by
Prabhu's grace. Why writhe now, and take suffering on yourself? Without fortune it does not come to
hand. Even now, live always in accord. Put pride away, and let the mind be low." With such words he
counselled Prithia, and much restrained him from quarrelling.

Description of the Episode of Giving the Guruship to Sri Arjan".*

### Notes

[^1]: The Elder is Baba Buddha Ji, who had served the Gurus from Guru Nanak Sahib Ji's time and who installed each new Guru in turn. He is called Buddha and Bhai the Elder further on. The word is capitalised where it designates him and left lowercase where it means an aged man.
[^2]: The chief jeweller is the Elder and the jewel is Sri Arjan; Bhai Vir Singh reads the appraising of this verse as his.
[^3]: Prithi Chand and Mahadev, the two elder sons of Guru Ram Das Ji, of whom Sri Arjan was the youngest brother. Prithi Chand is called Prithia through the rest of the ansu.
[^4]: Bibi Bhani Ji, wife of Guru Ram Das Ji; the father whose words she remembers is her own father, Guru Amar Das Ji.
[^5]: The golden world mountain of Indic cosmology, greatest of all peaks and proverbially immovable.
[^6]: Sant here is the Guru himself, the holy one the seeker is brought to.
[^7]: Prabhu: the Lord, one of the names of the divine used throughout the Guru Granth Sahib.
[^8]: Hari: another name of the divine, and the one most often on Guru Ram Das Ji's lips; Gupal, below, is a third.
[^9]: Prithia is throwing his father's own words back at him.
[^10]: The correspondence coming in to the Guru from the saṅgats, which Prithi Chand is said to have intercepted before it reached his father.
[^11]: Bhai Vir Singh's reading is that what Prithia calls seva, the running of the household and its management, was pride: he did it in pride, and there was no faith and no love in it at all, only greed for the seat, while Sri Arjan Dev Ji lived absorbed in the love of the Name and of the Satguru.
[^12]: The kalpataru of Indic cosmology, which gives whatever is asked of it. The verse says a crore of them, that is ten million.
