# Ras 2, Ansu 22

## The Guruship given to Sri Arjan Ji; the fourth pad of the shabad composed

*(Dohra)*

Hearing the Elder's[^1] words, anand[^2] rose in the Satguru's heart. *He has praised him, and so
Sri Arjan has become the moon of the family.* [1]

*(Chaupai)*

For many days now the Guruship had been carried on, and in that time the Elder had gained the eye
for appraising substance from worthlessness.[^3] When the chief jeweller appraises the jewel,[^4] it
becomes known to the whole world. [2]

A shrifal[^5] and five paise[^6] were sent for, and Sikhs were called in from wherever they were.
The whole sangat[^7] gathered, and closed around him on all four sides. [3] Everyone was watching.
*What will he do? Will he hold anger in his heart against the eldest son?* Then Sri Ram Das rose,
and took the shrifal and the paise in his hand. [4]

He made three pardakshan,[^8] recognising his son Sri Arjan as worthy. He set them down before him
and touched his forehead to the ground. There was great anand, an ocean of bibek.[^9] [5] Then, at
the Satguru's command, Buddha rose, gladdened in heart, and with his own hand applied the tilak[^10]
well on that fine forehead, which was filled with fortune.[^11] [6]

The Satguru spoke his word to them all. "Listen, Sikhs: you are all dear to the Guru. Look on Sri
Arjan as my own form; as you regard me, so hold him in your heart. [7] He is settled on the seat of
Guruship, and the whole burden of this world and the next is laid on him. He will carry the
sangat's suffering across, and accomplish many works in the world. [8] Rise, all of you, and make
your bandan[^12] to him. Give offerings of every kind."

Hearing the Satguru's words they rejoiced, and called out "Jai! Jai!" aloud. [9] Each man set gifts
before him as his means allowed, and bowed with folded hands. There was celebration among all the
Sikhs, and Sri Arjan was seen as the rising sun. [10]

The servants, a multitude of lotuses,[^13] opened; the seekers, a swarm of bees, were delighted. But
the hope in the hearts of Mahadev and Prithi,[^14] that night thick with the darkness of
bhram,[^15] was destroyed.[^16] [11] Their partisans, like stars, were hidden away, and the
slanderers, owls, shut their eyes and their mouths.[^17] Prithi Chand's brightness, which had been
like the moon's,[^18] went pale and dim in that very instant. [12]

He burned, and took great pain from it. He bent his neck; nothing was in his power. Mahadev stayed
silent and unmoved, making neither joy nor grief in his heart. [13]

When the mother heard it she felt herself greatly fortunate. *Arjan has become Guru*, and her
delight was great. Remembering her father's words she found ease. *The Guruship will not pass out of
the lineage. [14] What my father the Guru said is unshakeable. Sumeru[^19] may move; that will not
move.*[^20]

Then Sri Satguru took his seat in the assembly, the whole sangat around him on all four sides. [15]
Looking toward his son Sri Arjan, his mind especially glad, he spoke his word. "A shabad[^21] has
four pads[^22] in all. Three are made; one is wanted now. [16] Compose a fine pad here beside me,
and bring the shabad to its bhog,[^23] holding your mind steady, one that many Sikhs in the world
will read and hear, and take great joy in." [17]

The Elder,[^24] made glad, said, "The Satguru's word is fitting indeed." Sri Arjan heard it at that
moment, bowed to his father, and composed the pad. [18]

> ### Sri Mukhvaak
> *Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Raag Majh, Mahalla 4, Ang 96 to 97. The fourth pad, by Sri Guru Arjan Dev
> Ji, completing Guru Ram Das Ji's shabad* ਮੇਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਲੋਚੈ ਗੁਰ ਦਰਸਨ ਤਾਈ.
>
> ਭਾਗੁ ਹੋਆ ਗੁਰਿ ਸੰਤੁ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ ॥\
> *bhaag hoaa gur sant milaaiaa*\
> My fortune woke, and the Guru brought me to the Sant.
>
> ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਅਬਿਨਾਸੀ ਘਰ ਮਹਿ ਪਾਇਆ ॥\
> *prabh abinaasee ghar meh paaiaa*\
> I found the imperishable Prabhu inside my own house.
>
> ਸੇਵ ਕਰੀ ਪਲੁ ਚਸਾ ਨ ਵਿਛੁੜਾ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸ ਤੁਮਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ ॥੪॥\
> *sev karee pal chasaa na vichhurhaa jan naanak daas tumaare jeeo* ॥4॥\
> I do seva, and I am not parted from you for a moment or an instant; Nanak your servant, your slave.
>
> ਹਉ ਘੋਲੀ ਜੀਉ ਘੋਲਿ ਘੁਮਾਈ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸ ਤੁਮਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ ॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥੧॥੮॥\
> *hau gholee jeeo ghol ghumaaee jan naanak daas tumaare jeeo* ॥ *rahaao* ॥1॥8॥\
> I am a sacrifice, and my soul a sacrifice; Nanak your servant, your slave.

---

*(Chaupai)*

Sri Guru Ram Das was gladdened to hear it. *He will compose much bani*[^25] was his judgement. He
saw that the making of it was fine and clear, and that it had been composed at once, with no delay
taken. [19]

From his own mouth he instructed his son. "Make the shabad with humility, so that whoever reads it,
hears it and reflects on it has his hankar[^26] taken away, and his vices with it." [20]

Arjan heard his father's words with folded hands, and, wholly submissive, made his bandan. "You
alone are the doer and the cause of doing. I follow always as you say." [21]

When the assembly rose, each reached his own place, all of them saying, "This is good." Prithia met
with Bhai the Elder. He was burning, and nothing was in his power. [22]

"Listen, Buddha. We knew you. We held your greatness equal to Guru Nanak's, and such has my mind
been until today.[^27] No shardha[^28] remains now, having seen what you did.[^29] [23] You stayed
with the Satguru through it all, and watched the whole account from the beginning. I am the eldest,
and fully capable besides: the Guruship after my father was rightly mine. [24]

Of this you considered nothing. You have grown old, and the strength of your mind has failed
you.[^30] My father was Guru in name alone;[^31] I ran the greater part of the affairs. [25] You
know my competence in every kind of matter. The whole sangat took me for the Guru. What is this deed
you have suddenly done? You rose and gave the tilak at once. [26] If I overturn it by my own
strength, what honour of yours will be left? There was never any mention of this before. Why did you
do so improper a thing, and so suddenly? [27]

If my father, in anger, resolved on this and agreed to follow an improper practice, even then it was
fitting for you to counsel him and speak good sense: [28] 'This right belongs to the eldest son, and
he is fully capable in every way besides. All the father's affairs pass through him, and he manages
them with a generous good sense.' [29] Saying such things you should have counselled him, and kept
the Guruship from the younger son.[^32] That was the proper course for you. Then every mind would
have thought you wise. [30]

That good thing you did not do. You rose and gave the tilak on the instant. Will they not condemn
what you did, the wise Sikhs within the sangat? [31] I have seen your conduct. They call you a man
of sense, and you have done an unseemly thing.[^33] Now I will make constant effort. I will snatch
the seat away from him. [32] I will spend wealth in quantity and take it. What all of you have done,
I will now overturn."

The Elder said, "Why hold anger in your heart? You put no love into your father's seva.[^34] [33] Had
he been pleased he would have given it to you unasked, had he seen you worthy of it.[^35] By
backbiting it will not come to hand. Take hold of humility, and you get what you want. [34] You did
not properly accept your father's word, and that is why the tilak was not yours. You are a Guru's
son; great is your greatness; you are worthy of honour. What of it, if it did not come to
hand?[^36] [35] Leave anger and quarrelling, and find ease. As your father does, so gladden your
heart. He is master of faith and of the world: who can answer him back to his face?" [36]

Saying this, the Elder held his mouth in silence. Prithia went and entered the house. Speaking harsh
words, holding his anger, he came before his father, heavy with grievance. [37]

From his mouth he began to speak bitter words. "What contrary thing have you resolved on? You gave
what is mine to the younger. Who does such a thing? It has not happened before.[^37] [38] You did it
thinking ill of me. What fault did you see, that you cast me aside? The way you have dealt with me,
you will see the fruit of it in what comes.[^38] [39] Wealth in quantity is close at hand for me
now. I will join with whichever king is powerful, and by giving him wealth I will make him my own,
and show you suffering, father and son both.[^39] [40] When nothing at all is in your power, the
Guruship will be mine without my lifting a hand."

He raised much quarrelling of this kind. Upon this, Sri Guru uttered a shabad. [41]

> ### Sri Mukhvaak
> *Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Raag Sarang, Mahalla 4, Ghar 3, Dupada; Ang 1200.*
>
> ਸਾਰਗ ਮਹਲਾ ੪ ਘਰੁ ੩ ਦੁਪਦਾ\
> ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥\
> *saarag mahalaa 4 ghar 3 dupadaa · ik oankaar satigur prasaad*
>
> ਕਾਹੇ ਪੂਤ ਝਗਰਤ ਹਉ ਸੰਗਿ ਬਾਪ ॥\
> *kaahe poot jhagarat hau sang baap*\
> Why do you quarrel with your father, son?
>
> ਜਿਨ ਕੇ ਜਣੇ ਬਡੀਰੇ ਤੁਮ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਸਿਉ ਝਗਰਤ ਪਾਪ ॥੧॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥\
> *jin ke jane badeere tum hau tin siau jhagarat paap* ॥1॥ *rahaao* ॥\
> He fathered you and brought you up; to quarrel with him is sin.
>
> ਜਿਸੁ ਧਨ ਕਾ ਤੁਮ ਗਰਬੁ ਕਰਤ ਹਉ ਸੋ ਧਨੁ ਕਿਸਹਿ ਨ ਆਪ ॥\
> *jis dhan kaa tum garab karat hau so dhan kiseh na aap*\
> The wealth you are so proud of belongs to no one.
>
> ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਛੋਡਿ ਜਾਇ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਰਸੁ ਤਉ ਲਾਗੈ ਪਛੁਤਾਪ ॥੧॥\
> *khin meh chhod jaai bikhiaa ras tau laagai pachhutaap* ॥1॥\
> In an instant the taste of bikhia[^40] leaves you, and then the regret sets in.
>
> ਜੋ ਤੁਮਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਹੋਤੇ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਹਰਿ ਤਿਨ ਕੇ ਜਾਪਹੁ ਜਾਪ ॥\
> *jo tumare prabh hote suaamee har tin ke jaapahu jaap*\
> Say the Name of Hari over, who was your Prabhu and your master.
>
> ਉਪਦੇਸੁ ਕਰਤ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਨ ਤੁਮ ਕਉ ਜਉ ਸੁਨਹੁ ਤਉ ਜਾਇ ਸੰਤਾਪ ॥੨॥੧॥੭॥\
> *updes karat naanak jan tum kau jau sunahu tau jaai santaap* ॥2॥1॥7॥\
> Nanak his servant gives you this updesh: listen to it, and your torment goes.

---

*(Chaupai)*

"Prithia, listen. What pride is there in wealth, which forsakes a man and does not stay an instant
for anyone?[^41] Regret is what is left behind. Quarrelling with a father brings no honour. [42] So
do simran[^42] of Hari, Hari, and hold this good updesh.[^43] All the torments of body and mind are
destroyed by it, and greed and grave sin take no hold again." [43]

Hearing it, Prithia was greatly galled again. "'Do simran of the Name of Hari, Hari, Gupal'[^44] you
say, after giving the Guruship to the younger son and dishonouring me in front of everyone. [44]
Seeing me fit only to be preached at, you have picked a fine moment to speak. 'Put the hope of
Guruship out of your heart,[^45] and worship Hari, becoming a servant.' [45] This updesh of yours I
shall take up![^46] Fine work you will make of carrying me across the ocean of existence! You do
what is unfit and consider nothing, and now you say, 'Do simran of Hari, Hari.'" [46]

Sri Ram Das heard those crooked words. *He will not turn back, however anyone restrains him;[^47]
his one love is in the doing of wrong.*[^48] Then he composed another shabad and recited it. [47]

> ### Sri Mukhvaak
> *Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Raag Suhi, Mahalla 4; Ang 733.*
>
> ਸੂਹੀ ਮਹਲਾ ੪ ॥\
> *soohee mahalaa 4*
>
> ਤਿਨ੍ਹੀ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਆਰਾਧਿਆ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਧੁਰਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਲਿਖਤੁ ਲਿਲਾਰਾ ॥\
> *tinhee antar har aaraadhiaa jin kau dhur likhiaa likhat lilaaraa*\
> Only they worship Hari within, on whose foreheads it was written from the beginning.
>
> ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਬਖੀਲੀ ਕੋਈ ਕਿਆ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਨ ਕਾ ਅੰਗੁ ਕਰੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਕਰਤਾਰਾ ॥੧॥\
> *tin kee bakheelee koee kiaa kare jin kaa ang kare meraa har kartaaraa* ॥1॥\
> What can anyone's backbiting do to them, when Hari my Creator takes their side?
>
> ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਇ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਧਿਆਇ ਹਰਿ ਜਨਮ ਜਨਮ ਕੇ ਸਭਿ ਦੂਖ ਨਿਵਾਰਣਹਾਰਾ ॥੧॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥\
> *har har dhiaai man mere man dhiaai har janam janam ke sabh dookh nivaaranahaaraa* ॥1॥ *rahaao* ॥\
> Meditate on Hari, Hari, my mind; meditate on Hari, my mind; he takes away all the suffering of birth after birth.
>
> ਧੁਰਿ ਭਗਤ ਜਨਾ ਕਉ ਬਖਸਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਭਗਤਿ ਭੰਡਾਰਾ ॥\
> *dhur bhagat janaa kau bakhasiaa har amrit bhagat bhandaaraa*\
> From the beginning Hari granted his bhagats the amrit treasury of bhagti.
>
> ਮੂਰਖੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੁ ਉਨ ਕੀ ਰੀਸ ਕਰੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਹਲਤਿ ਪਲਤਿ ਮੁਹੁ ਕਾਰਾ ॥੨॥\
> *moorakh hovai su un kee rees kare tis halat palat muhu kaaraa* ॥2॥\
> Whoever competes with them is a fool; his face is blackened in this world and the next.
>
> ਸੇ ਭਗਤ ਸੇ ਸੇਵਕਾ ਜਿਨਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ ॥\
> *se bhagat se sevakaa jinaa har naam piaaraa*\
> They are the bhagats, they are the sevaks, to whom Hari's Name is dear.
>
> ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਿਰਿ ਨਿੰਦਕ ਕੈ ਪਵੈ ਛਾਰਾ ॥੩॥\
> *tin kee sevaa te har paaeeai sir nindak kai pavai chhaaraa* ॥3॥\
> Hari is found through their seva; ashes fall on the slanderer's head.
>
> ਜਿਸੁ ਘਰਿ ਵਿਰਤੀ ਸੋਈ ਜਾਣੈ ਜਗਤ ਗੁਰ ਨਾਨਕ ਪੂਛਿ ਕਰਹੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਾ ॥\
> *jis ghar viratee soee jaanai jagat gur naanak poochh karahu beechaaraa*\
> Only the man in whose own house it works knows it. Ask Nanak, Guru of the world, and reflect on it.
>
> ਚਹੁ ਪੀੜੀ ਆਦਿ ਜੁਗਾਦਿ ਬਖੀਲੀ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਇਓ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਵਕ ਭਾਇ ਨਿਸਤਾਰਾ ॥੪॥੨॥੯॥\
> *chahu peerhee aad jugaad bakheelee kinai na paaio har sevak bhaai nistaaraa* ॥4॥2॥9॥\
> Through four generations, from the beginning of the ages, no one has gained by backbiting; deliverance comes by the servant's love.

---

*(Chaupai)*

"The liv[^49] of Hari is fixed within him, for whom good fortune is written from the beginning. Sri
Arjan's helper, always, is Hari the Creator, lord of the whole world. [48] What backbiting can you do
against him, in whom the sweet liv of Hari is fixed? The bhagat's blessing too was seen from the
beginning, and the amrit treasury of bhagti[^50] is in his use. [49]

Whoever competes with them is a fool: his face is blackened in this world and the next. He to whom
Hari's Name and bhagti are dear is a servant, and through such men the light of Prabhu is
obtained. [50] Nothing comes to the slanderer's hand. Ashes fall on his head, and afterwards he
repents. The man in whose heart the liv of Hari is at work, he is the one who knows the Satguru's
greatness. [51]

Sri Nanak; Guru Angad, the moon; Sri Guru Amar Das, the root of ease: the fourth generation[^51] of
the Guruship has reached me, and all the ages have watched it. [52] By backbiting it did not come to
your hand.[^52] The supreme state is gained by doing seva,[^53] and that never once came near
you.[^54] Filled with pride, your mind was steeped in deceit. [53]

You took me for a man of little knowledge. You practised deceit, holding yourself great.[^55]
Holding envy, you took the letters[^56] and hid them away, as an enemy would.[^57] [54] We kept
questioning you, and you would not admit it. Fearless, in bad mind, you swore an oath.[^58] You
counted the fault of a false oath as nothing, and went on taking me for a man who did not
know. [55]

Without humility there is no seva. Where is seva? There is only great egotism.[^59] Shardha is the
great root of it all, and love is the sprout,[^60] the giver of ease. [56] Seva is the bark, which
makes it firm;[^61] vairag[^62] the great branches, spreading wide;[^63] Hari's Name blossoms out of
it as flowers;[^64] and in the sat sangat it sways, and is beautiful.[^65] [57]

The many virtues of daivi sampada[^66] are its leaves, that mass of virtues strong with bibek.[^67]
Atam gyan[^68] hangs on it as beautiful fruit, with great anand as the juice inside it.[^69] [58] A
crore of kalpataru[^70] are not its equal.[^71] By Prabhu's grace a man finds it within himself. Why
writhe now, and take suffering on yourself? Without fortune it does not come to hand. [59]

Even now, live always in accord. Take away hankar;[^72] let the mind be low." With such words
Prithia was counselled, and much restrained from quarrelling. [60]

Description of the Episode of Giving the Guruship to Sri Arjan."*

### Translator's flags

The text is translated as an 1843 composition, on its own terms. Prithi Chand's accusations against
Baba Buddha Ji and Guru Ram Das Ji (vv. 23 to 32, 38 to 41, 44 to 46) are rendered at full force,
and the Guru's replies are not softened toward him.

- **Gurbani English.** Rendered here in the same modern register as the surrounding prose, informed
  by Dr Sant Singh Khalsa's translation. **The Fareedkot Teeka could not be reached** from this
  session; srigranth.org and searchgurbani.com did not serve its column, so Sant Singh is the only
  translation consulted, as agreed. Worth a second pass if you get the teeka in front of you.
- **Majh, ਭਾਗੁ ਹੋਆ ਗੁਰਿ ਸੰਤੁ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ.** Two readings of ਗੁਰਿ ਸੰਤੁ: "the Guru brought me to the Sant"
  (taken here, and in the verse file) or "I met the Saint-Guru" (Sant Singh). Both are defensible;
  the line is not disputed in substance.
- **Suhi, closing numbering.** Our source prints ॥੪॥੨॥੯॥. srigranth.org now also reports ॥੪॥੨॥੯॥,
  against the ॥੪॥੧॥੮॥ noted in the verse file's flags, but that reading came back from a fetch in
  which both forms were named in the query, so treat it as weak confirmation, not settled. The one
  look at a printed Granth is still worth doing.
- **v. 26** ਕਿੱ੍ਰਤਿ is printed thus in the source; read here as ਕ੍ਰਿਤਿ, "deed".
- **vv. 34, 47** ਹੋਟੇ and ਖੁਟਾਈ are obscure; the readings given follow the sense of the passage.
- **v. 53** ਭਰੇ ਗਰਬ ਸੋਂ ਮਤ ਛਲ ਭੋਈ. "your mind was steeped in deceit" takes ਭੋਈ as from ਭੋਣਾ, to be
  soaked. Worth one check against the Aulakh Steek.
- The Gurbani quotations are reproduced as the source prints them; where the source's orthography
  differs from the Granth's it has been left as printed, not silently corrected.
